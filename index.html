    Fusion rules

    Chess as usual, with one extra move: fuse two of your pieces into one.

    Fuse

    Move a piece onto another of the same color (violet square). They become one unit with the moves of both. Fusing uses up your turn.

    Limits

    At most two pieces per square. A fused unit cannot fuse again. The King is left out.

    Absolute unit

    Fusion is permanent. The unit moves together and is captured together: both pieces leave the board at once.

    Promotion

    If a unit containing a pawn reaches the last rank, only the pawn is promoted. The other piece stays the same.
